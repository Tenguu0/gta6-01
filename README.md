An original, compact crime adventure set in Brackwater, a rain-worn harbor town built around a failing floodwall. Courier Mara Venn has one night to trace a missing ledger through three jobs while neighbors, patrol cars and the rising water close in.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in a modern browser. The game is fully local and needs no install, network access or build step.

- **WASD / arrow keys:** walk or steer
- **E:** take a nearby car, leave it, or use a nearby job marker
- **Mouse click:** fire; shots raise the heat
- **Space:** brake while driving
- **Esc:** save locally and pause; choose Continue to resume

Find a vehicle, reach the glowing markers in order, and keep moving when patrol cars arrive. The map, current job, cash and heat stay visible while you explore.

## Entry files

`main.ts` copies the static game and captures into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and writes the make.fun dossier. Screenshots are in `screenshots/`.
